# Glacial ice

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Nature & material). Also known as arctic blue, glacier, polar minimal, frozen.

## Overview

Pale glacier-blue ground, a frosted-ice panel holding one giant sub-zero figure, a cold glint crossing it and clean Sora numerals.

It feels crisp, pure, still. Use it for climate and science films, water and beverage brands, winter sports, cooling and hardware launches.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Sora (display, weight 600, tracking -0.04em) with IBM Plex Sans for body text.
- **Surfaces:** translucent frosted panels (backdrop blur) over colour; corners 22px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** float shadows (`0 40px 70px -16px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).
- **Texture:** none; clean fills. **Icons:** line. **Decoration:** none.

## Motion

Motion language: **Weightless**. Zero-gravity motion: objects drift at constant slow velocity and keep rotating, with no floor, no settling and no rest point.
- Enter `power1.out`, exit `power1.in`, move `none`; durations 600 / 1000 / 1600 / 2400 / 3600 ms; stagger 120 ms; hold at least 1600 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, bounce, overshoot, scale-pop, linear-entrance.
- Preview entrance: drift.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#5fb0d4) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Sora large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Do keep every shadow the same float style.
- Don't mix surface treatments; everything is glass.
- Don't confuse it: Not light glassmorphism: no colourful blurred blobs behind glass; one cold blue family, ice and snow light, and data at the centre.
