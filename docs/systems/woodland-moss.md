# Woodland moss

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Nature & material). Also known as forest floor, moss green aesthetic, woodland, forest bathing.

## Overview

Deep moss-green understory, spores and dappled light out of focus behind a seasons timeline, chanterelle-gold marker, Alegreya serif.

It feels hushed, alive, rooted. Use it for outdoor and conservation films, wellness and retreats, nature documentaries, sustainability reports.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Alegreya (display, weight 700, tracking -0.01em) with Alegreya Sans for body text.
- **Surfaces:** flat fills, no gradients; corners 14px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** no shadows.
- **Texture:** grain. **Icons:** filled. **Decoration:** none.

## Motion

Motion language: **Organic**. Motion that follows living, growing systems: soft sine curves, arcs rather than straight lines, uneven timing and growth from a source.
- Enter `sine.out`, exit `sine.in`, move `sine.inOut`; durations 400 / 650 / 1000 / 1500 / 2200 ms; stagger 90 ms; hold at least 1100 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, bounce, scale-pop, linear-entrance.
- Preview entrance: drift.

## The terms that define it

- **Visual style / art direction:** Cinematic, Documentary
- **Typography:** Editorial Serif, Humanist Sans
- **Composition / layout system:** Timeline
- **Color treatment:** Earth Tones, Dark UI
- **Texture:** Film Grain
- **Lighting:** Atmospheric Haze
- **VFX / compositing treatment:** Depth of Field
- **Motion language:** Organic
- **Pacing / rhythm:** Slow
- **Transition language:** Dissolve, Camera Move Transition
- **Emotional / brand tone:** Calm, Mysterious

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#93b24f) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Alegreya large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Don't add drop shadows.
- Don't use gradients or glassy surfaces.
- Don't confuse it: Not solarpunk: no optimistic tech or bright greens; dark, damp, quiet forest with the light filtered through the canopy.
