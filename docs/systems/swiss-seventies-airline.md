# 70s airline modernism

A motion-graphics design system from Rasa Director's style library (Retro & eras). Also known as Pan Am style, jet-age corporate, Swiss 70s airline, route-map modernism.

## Overview

Crisp grotesk on cream, bold red and navy stripes, route-map nodes and connectors, orderly Swiss-grid information design.

It feels confident, orderly, optimistic. Use it for travel and logistics, process explainers, corporate brand films, fintech.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Archivo (display, weight 800, tracking -0.03em) with Archivo for body text.
- **Surfaces:** flat fills, no gradients; corners 4px; outlines 3px solid in ink.
- **Depth:** no shadows.
- **Texture:** none; clean fills. **Icons:** line. **Decoration:** rules.

## Motion

Motion language: **Precise**. Designed, exact motion with strong exponential deceleration, grid-locked destinations and zero overshoot.
- Enter `expo.out`, exit `expo.in`, move `expo.inOut`; durations 200 / 300 / 450 / 700 / 1000 ms; stagger 80 ms; hold at least 900 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, fade-slide, bounce, overshoot, blur-in, opacity-only-entrance.
- Preview entrance: slide.

## The terms that define it

- **Visual style / art direction:** Swiss / International Typographic Style, Modernist
- **Era / design-movement influence:** Swiss / International Typographic Style, 1970s
- **Composition / layout system:** Swiss Grid, Flowchart
- **Information / data visualization:** Flow Diagram, Map
- **Typography:** Grotesk
- **Color treatment:** Limited Palette
- **Motion language:** Precise
- **Transition language:** Line-Draw Transition, Wipe

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#d62828) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Archivo large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Don't add drop shadows.
- Don't use gradients or glassy surfaces.
- Don't confuse it: Not modern Swiss minimal: warm cream paper, livery stripes and jet-age optimism, not white SaaS.
