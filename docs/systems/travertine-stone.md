# Travertine stone

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Nature & material). Also known as travertine, honed stone, Roman travertine, limestone minimal.

## Overview

Honed travertine: pitted warm-beige stone, a slab of darker vein, wide-set Italiana capitals over Jost, a single column of air.

It feels sunlit, architectural, unhurried. Use it for architecture and interiors, hotels and spas, furniture launches, fragrance and ceramics.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Italiana (display, weight 400, uppercase, tracking 0.06em) with Jost for body text.
- **Surfaces:** flat fills, no gradients; corners 0px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** no shadows.
- **Texture:** noise. **Icons:** none. **Decoration:** none.

## Motion

Motion language: **Luxurious / Slow**. Very slow, circular deceleration, long holds and stillness, where restraint itself is the signal of value.
- Enter `circ.out`, exit `circ.in`, move `power1.inOut`; durations 600 / 900 / 1400 / 2000 / 2800 ms; stagger 200 ms; hold at least 1800 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, fade-slide, bounce, overshoot, scale-pop, linear-entrance, blur-in.
- Preview entrance: fade.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#c2ab8e) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Italiana large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Don't add drop shadows.
- Don't use gradients or glassy surfaces.
- Don't confuse it: Not marble and gold: no metallic accents or polish; porous, matte stone in one warm family, the luxury is the material.
