# Metal band tee

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Music & scene). Also known as heavy metal merch, blackletter band logo, black metal shirt, tour tee print.

## Overview

Screen-printed band shirt: white blackletter on pitch-black cotton, one blood-red fleuron, cracked plastisol grain over everything.

It feels menacing, raw, loyal. Use it for metal and rock releases, tour announcements, merch drops, horror and gaming trailers.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Pirata One (display, weight 400, tracking 0.01em) with Oswald for body text.
- **Surfaces:** flat fills, no gradients; corners 0px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** no shadows.
- **Texture:** grain. **Icons:** glyph. **Decoration:** none.

## Motion

Motion language: **Heavy**. Motion that communicates large mass: slow to start, accelerating under gravity, landing with impact and minimal rebound.
- Enter `power4.in`, exit `power3.in`, move `power3.inOut`; durations 120 / 250 / 450 / 800 / 1200 ms; stagger 120 ms; hold at least 900 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, fade-slide, bounce, overshoot, scale-pop, blur-in, opacity-only-entrance.
- Preview entrance: snap.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#b3121b) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Pirata One large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Don't add drop shadows.
- Don't use gradients or glassy surfaces.
- Don't confuse it: Not gothic luxury: nothing is polished or gold; it's cheap, cracked screen-print ink on a black tee with aggressive letterforms.
