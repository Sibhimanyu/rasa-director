# Cyanotype

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Nature & material). Also known as sun print, blueprint photogram, Anna Atkins, Prussian blue print.

## Overview

A Prussian-blue sun print: landscape and silhouettes exposed pale on deep cyan paper, uneven emulsion, an IM Fell English title.

It feels dreamlike, historic, handmade. Use it for art and photography films, botanical and science stories, album and music visuals, heritage brand films.

## Visual language

- **Type:** IM Fell English (display, weight 400, tracking -0.01em) with Courier Prime for body text.
- **Surfaces:** flat fills, no gradients; corners 0px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** no shadows.
- **Texture:** paper. **Icons:** none. **Decoration:** none.

## Motion

Motion language: **Organic**. Motion that follows living, growing systems: soft sine curves, arcs rather than straight lines, uneven timing and growth from a source.
- Enter `sine.out`, exit `sine.in`, move `sine.inOut`; durations 400 / 650 / 1000 / 1500 / 2200 ms; stagger 90 ms; hold at least 1100 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, bounce, scale-pop, linear-entrance.
- Preview entrance: fade.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#e4edf7) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use IM Fell English large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Don't add drop shadows.
- Don't use gradients or glassy surfaces.
- Don't confuse it: Not blueprint schematic: no technical line drawing or grid; a photographic chemical print with soft, sunlit tones.
