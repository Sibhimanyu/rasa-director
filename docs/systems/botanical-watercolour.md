# Botanical watercolour plate

A motion-graphics design system from RasanAI's style library (Nature & material). Also known as botanical illustration, watercolour botanical, flora plate, herbal watercolour.

## Overview

Loose sap-green and rose-madder washes bleeding at the edges of cold-press paper, a Cormorant Garamond title on a deckled plate, pressed-flower icons.

It feels gentle, natural, cultivated. Use it for skincare and apothecary brands, garden and florist films, wedding and stationery, tea and wellness launches.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display, weight 600, tracking -0.01em) with EB Garamond for body text.
- **Surfaces:** off-white paper with visible fibre; corners 6px; outlines none.
- **Depth:** soft shadows (`0 18px 44px rgba(0,0,0,0.16)`).
- **Texture:** paper. **Icons:** duotone. **Decoration:** blobs.

## Motion

Motion language: **Organic**. Motion that follows living, growing systems: soft sine curves, arcs rather than straight lines, uneven timing and growth from a source.
- Enter `sine.out`, exit `sine.in`, move `sine.inOut`; durations 400 / 650 / 1000 / 1500 / 2200 ms; stagger 90 ms; hold at least 1100 ms.
- Never: fade-up-slide, bounce, scale-pop, linear-entrance.
- Preview entrance: fade.

- **Visual style / art direction:** Handmade / Craft, Warm Minimalism
- **Illustration style:** Watercolor
- **Typography:** Editorial Serif

- **Material / surface language:** Paper

- **Transition language:** Dissolve, Mask Reveal
- **Emotional / brand tone:** Calm, Premium

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the accent (#8fb07a) for the single most important element in each frame.
- Do use Cormorant Garamond large and confident; one idea per frame.
- Do keep every shadow the same soft style.
- Don't mix surface treatments; everything is paper.
- Don't confuse it: Not flat watercolour: that is a bright kids'-book wash behind a hand font; this is a scientific flora plate with a classical serif and muted pigments.
