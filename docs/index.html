Rasa Director is a Claude Code skill for entire videos: launch films, explainers, PR videos, music videos, reels cut from your own footage, motion graphics. You make four calls and see your film at each one. Claude makes every craft decision, lists each with its reason, and checks the film for AI-video tells before you see it.

One four-scene film, six motion languages. Swatches to choose by eye; the real film is Claude's.

Thirty people make an AI video. They get thirty videos and one film.

The content differs. The film is the same: the obvious story, a default look, everything fading up on one ease, a 30-second music loop under a 45-second film, a whoosh on every cut, glowing text. Viewers now name these tells on sight.

Each one is a default nobody chose. Rasa Director replaces every default with a decision, then checks the build against all of them.

How it runs

Four calls. You see your film at every one.

You type /rasa-director make a 45s launch film for tally.app. A Director's Console opens in your browser, shows what Claude is doing as it works, and stops you four times.

  1. 1

    Brief

    One sentence you can edit: "A 45-second launch film for Tally, 16:9 for the website, with voiceover, in Tally's own colours and type", and what Claude captured from the site.

    Your callFix a word and press Start, or press Just make it.

  2. 2

    Films

    Three complete films. Each is a named design system, drawn live and moving on the film's own opening line, with a story angle (Sure, Bold or Wild), a logline and its music feel.

    Your callPick one, or ask for more like these, mix two, turn the energy up or down, or browse every style.

  3. 3

    Animatic

    The whole film, rough: every scene's key frame at its real duration, with the real music edit and the voice.

    Your callPlay it and click any moment to leave a note. Then Claude builds it scene by scene while you watch.

  4. 4

    Final

    The rendered film with scene markers, your notes, every version and what changed between them.

    Your callNote a moment, apply the notes, render and download.

Claude decides the rest.

The craft is decided the way a senior motion designer would decide it, from a quantified playbook, and every call is listed in the Decisions drawer with its reason. Change any of it by telling Claude (⌘K) or leaving a note.

Under the four calls

What makes the films different.

A story picked from what is true

Before any concept, Claude writes a truth sheet from your product: what really changes, for whom, in its own words and numbers. From 66 narrative devices it pitches three films that differ on at least five of seven axes. A rubric rejects the default arc, and any pitch that would work for any product.

ExampleFor a bookkeeping app, The Museum Exhibit: the shoebox of receipts under glass (c. 2019), a placard for every late-night spreadsheet, and an empty plinth where tax season used to be.

403 design systems

Named styles in 20 families, from Swiss grids and risograph zines to split-flap departure boards. Each is a stack of taxonomy terms, a recipe drawn live on your words, a motion contract and a complete DESIGN.md. Claude matches the system to the story.

ExampleMuseum wall text: Instrument Sans on a pale gallery wall, one ultramarine field, no shadows, circ.out entrances of 600 to 2800 ms.

Music edited to the picture

Tracks long enough for the film, analysed with nothing but Node and FFmpeg for beats, bars, sections and a real ending, then cut on bar lines so the music never loops. The reveal and the logo land on downbeats. Sound effects only for visible events, on a budget. Mixed to −14 LUFS.

ExampleA 45-second film gets a track that ends at 45 seconds: one forward jump on a bar line into the real ending, and half a bar of near-silence before the reveal.

An anti-slop gate

Before anything reaches you, slop.mjs checks five kinds of tell. Copy: seamless, unlock, the not-X-it's-Y line, em dashes. Look: neon glow text, purple-blue gradients, particle filler, corner timecodes, Inter everywhere. Motion: idle loops, everything entering the same way. Timing: unreadable text, uniform shots, no end hold. Sound: loops, a whoosh per cut, loudness, dead air.

Examplegeneric-copy · error on the headline Unlock seamless bookkeeping. Fix: the real outcome, number or feature, in the product's own words.

The style library

403 design systems. Pick one by eye.

Every style has a proper name and sits in one of 20 families: bold, soft, editorial, retro, future, handmade, 3D and materials, product, data, cinematic, playful, luxury, heritage, broadcast, science, print, interface, nature, music and scene, place and wayfinding. Each is drawn live on your own words.

Every style is a complete design system: colors by role, type, radii, shadows, components, the motion contract, and what to do and avoid. Download any card as a DESIGN.md and any agent or tool can build from it. With your DESIGN.md, the styles are drawn in your colors and type. Tap a card to see it move.

The whole library as files: systems/index.json lists every style with its DESIGN.md. Inside Claude Code, ask for any style by name: /rasa-director export the Swiss grid style as a DESIGN.md.

Your brand

Bring your DESIGN.md.

If your project has a brand reference, it becomes the look. All three films are drawn in its colors and type, and the brief sentence says so. Ask for a new look and the styles use their own colors instead.

  • Reads the common shapes: design.md spec frontmatter with oklch() colors and nested typography, impeccable and gstack files, Stitch-style prose, CSS custom properties, token tables with or without #.
  • Gives every color a role (canvas, ink, accent, surface, muted, status) from its name and comment, then contrast. A saturated "text accent" never becomes the ink; status colors never become the accent.
  • Finds the fonts, skips "banned" ones, maps platform fonts the renderer doesn't have (SF Pro → Inter, SF Mono → JetBrains Mono, Calibri → Carlito) and says so.
  • Writes a frame.md HyperFrames accepts, checks it with HyperFrames' own parser, and stages every font file into the project.

See an example DESIGN.md

The vocabulary

A film is a stack of decisions.

Two pieces can both be "2D UI animation" and look nothing alike. Rasa holds the vocabulary of motion design as data: 32 dimensions and 926 terms, each with a definition, what it looks like, how it moves, where it's used, what it's confused with, and the instruction Claude follows.

Every decision Claude makes, and every prompt to a builder, is written in these terms. A film vocabulary turns each one (a rack focus, a bleach-bypass grade, a J-cut) into an HTML, CSS and GSAP recipe with numbers. Share a screenshot or a video you like and Claude reads it into the same terms.

The motion

A motion language, with a contract.

"If these graphics were alive, what laws would they follow?" Each of the 32 motion languages is a term with a binding contract: its eases, the durations allowed, the stagger, the minimum hold, and the moves it bans. The chosen style's language becomes motion.md.

Every language's swatch passes the same checker the real build must pass, so what you see is what's enforced. One language governs the whole film; scenes differ only in intensity.

Preciseexpo.out / expo.in · 200–1000 ms · stagger 80 · hold 900Exact, grid-locked destinations, zero overshoot.
Springyback.out(1.7) / power3.in · 180–1100 ms · stagger 40A damped spring: fast attack, one overshoot, quick settle.
Cinematicpower2.out / power2.inOut · 400–2400 ms · hold 1400Slow push-ins and breathing holds.
Snappypower4.out / power3.in · 120–720 ms · stagger 50Steep initial velocity, very short settle.
Luxurious / slowcirc.out / circ.in · 600–2800 ms · hold 1800Restraint as the signal of value.
Stop-motion-likesteps(6) / steps(4) · 12 fps · hold 750Held poses on twos, like physical stop-motion.

Who animates

Claude designs and animates every frame.

Rasa never ships canned animation. The style specimens exist so you can choose by eye. The frames, the scenes and the cards are Claude's, drawn fresh from the chosen film.

For the animatic, Claude designs one key frame per scene, the moment that scene is about, and times them to the music before anything moves. Then it builds every scene through the matching HyperFrames workflow, from DIRECTION.md, frame.md and motion.md, and you watch each one turn from key frame to finished.

  1. FilmA story device and a design system, compiled into DIRECTION.md and motion.md
  2. Key framesOne per scene, timed to the music edit and the voice
  3. BuildEvery scene worker reads the direction and the contract in its packet
  4. GatesAnti-slop, the motion contract and the sound check, on every scene
  5. FinalRender, notes on any moment, versions

Footage

Point it at a folder of clips.

Rasa examines every clip (length, orientation, frame rate, sound, a contact sheet, shot changes, silences and a word-level transcript), then Claude drafts the cut, cutting on word boundaries.

A reel adds two calls: Footage, where you untick clips to leave out, and Cut, the reel's animatic, where you edit the list: in and out points, order, cards between clips, overlays, captions from what's said, a music bed that ducks under speech. Claude designs and animates every card and overlay.

The checks

Checked before you see it.

A film ships only after four gates pass: the story rubric, the anti-slop gate, the motion contract and the sound check. The motion check walks every tween of every scene in a real browser and compares it with the contract.

  • Durations on the language's scale, eases from its set, written explicitly, never the GSAP default.
  • Banned moves caught in every form, including the generic fade-up-slide.
  • Holds long enough to read; GSAP only, seekable, deterministic.
  • Sound at −14 LUFS, no audible loop, effects within budget, no dead air.
  • Failures go back to the builder, at most twice; anything left is yours to fix or waive at the Final. A check that couldn't run is never reported as a pass.

Install

Needs Claude Code, Node.js 20 or newer (Rasa fetches a private copy if you have none), the HyperFrames skills (npx hyperframes skills update), and FFmpeg for music and footage.

Claude Code plugin

From inside Claude Code.

> /plugin marketplace add Sibhimanyu/rasa-director
> /plugin install rasa-director@rasa-director

skills CLI

From your terminal.

$ npx skills add Sibhimanyu/rasa-director --skill rasa-director

One-line installer

Clones and links the skill.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sibhimanyu/rasa-director/master/install.sh | bash

Then try

> /rasa-director make a 45s launch film for https://tally.app
> /rasa-director a 60s explainer on how DNS works, vertical, no voiceover. Just make it.
> /rasa-director cut ~/Footage/lisbon into a 30s reel with a title card and captions
> /rasa-director export the Swiss grid style as a DESIGN.md

Rasa updates itself when the skill loads. Check the install with node ~/.claude/skills/rasa-director/scripts/selftest.mjs (84 checks, no network needed).

Questions

What do I actually decide?

Four things: the brief (one sentence), which of three complete films to make, notes on the animatic, and the final. Everything else is craft, and Claude decides it. If a question would need expertise to answer, Rasa doesn't ask it.

Can I change what Claude decided?

Yes. Every call is in the Decisions drawer with its reason and the obvious option it passed over. Tell Claude (⌘K) or leave a note on a moment in the animatic or the final, and it changes only what the note points at.

Who actually animates the video?

Claude. The style specimens in the console are for choosing by eye. Claude designs the key frames, the scenes and the reel cards from the chosen film, HyperFrames renders them, and the gates confirm the motion, copy and sound before you see the result.

Do I need to know motion-design terms?

No. You choose by watching and listening. Claude works in the terms, because they make the result precise: "simplified UI, grotesk, precise motion, UI-morph transitions" builds something very different from "make it modern".

Why do the three films look so different from each other?

Because they are different films. Each pairs a different story device with a design system from a different family and its own music feel, and the three pitches differ on at least five of seven story axes. Your recent styles and devices rotate out, so ten films never settle into one house style.

Can I use a style outside Rasa?

Yes. Every style in the library downloads as a DESIGN.md (colors by role, type, radii, shadows, components, the motion contract), which any agent or design tool can build from. The link is on each card above.

Can it use my brand?

Yes. Put your DESIGN.md (or design.md) in the project. Rasa reads it, gives every color a role, and draws all three films in your colors and type.

Can I skip everything?

Yes. Say "just make it" and Claude decides every call up to the Final, which always runs so you can review before the render. Only the source (what the video is about) is required.

Does anything leave my machine?

The console runs on 127.0.0.1 with a per-session token and serves files only from your workspace. Music analysis runs locally with Node and FFmpeg. Site capture, voice samples, music and fonts go through HyperFrames' tools, Google Fonts or Fontshare; nothing else leaves your machine.
