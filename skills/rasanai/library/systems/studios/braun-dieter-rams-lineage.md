## What it is
Braun's modern design identity began in the mid-1950s, when the company set up a design department under Fritz Eichler and worked with the Ulm School of Design (Wikipedia). Dieter Rams joined in 1955 as an architect and interior designer, became Design Director in 1962 (Design Museum; Wikipedia says head of design from 1961 and until 1997 or 1995 depending on the page, so the exact dates conflict), and retired in 1995, succeeded by Peter Schneider. Key objects: the SK 4 radiogram (1956, "Snow White's Coffin", with Hans Gugelot), the T3 pocket radio (1958), the Audio 1 system (1962), Studio 1000 (1965, introduced a black anthracite finish), the T2 lighter (1968), the ET22 calculator (1977) and the ET66 (1987, with Dietrich Lubs). Rams also designed the 606 shelving for Vitsoe from 1959. The line runs on to Apple's Jony Ive.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
Rams's ten principles (Vitsoe's wording): innovative; useful; aesthetic; understandable; unobtrusive; honest; long-lasting; thorough ("nothing must be arbitrary or left to chance"); environmentally friendly; as little design as possible ("less, but better").
- Colour as a signal. HueAtlas decodes the palette: Braun White #f0ede5 as primary ground, Braun Grey #8a8a87 for dials and grilles, Anthracite #2e2e2c, Pebble #c5c3be, Warm Black #1a1a18 for type only, Signal Red #c02820 and Function Yellow #d4b018, never both accents on one surface.
- The ET66 (V&A): black plastic, grey and black number keys, brown operator keys, a yellow equals key because it is the most used; 7.8 x 13.6 x 1.1 cm, sliding protective sleeve. Colour follows frequency of use.
- Modular sizing, minimal buttons in orderly arrangement, colour-coded with white, grey, black (Design Museum).
- 1970s: pop influences gave "bright colors and a lightness of touch while still being clean-lined" (Wikipedia, Braun). Use sparingly.

## Motion and camera
Braun published products, not motion; the timings are proposed, derived from the rule that only the function moves.

2D (HyperFrames, craft.md vocabulary): everything still except the function.
- A control lights (opacity 0 to 1 in 0.2 s `power2.out`), a dial rotates 0 to 72 degrees over 0.9 s `power2.inOut` (transform-origin at the dial centre), a drawer or sleeve slides 40 px over 0.6 s `power2.inOut` (the ET66 has a sliding protective sleeve, per V&A). Labels fade 12 frames after the control at 30 fps (0.4 s). Holds are long: 1.2 to 2.4 s.
- No bounce, no blur, no glow beyond a 6 px soft indicator light. Camera: locked, or a 4 percent push over 6 s `sine.inOut`. Grade: neutral, a touch warm to keep the #f0ede5 ground. Sound: mechanical clicks (switch, dial detent) on the action frame, no music swells.
3D (Rasan3D, 3d.md sections 3, 5, 18): the object working.
- Body: `k.panel({ width: 1.36, height: 0.78, depth: 0.11, radius: 0.012, texture: k.image(<face png>), bodyColor: "#2b2b2b" })` for a calculator-like slab with an unlit exact face, or boxes with `k.material("matte", { color: "#2e2e2c", roughness: 0.6 })` and keys `k.material("plastic", { color: "#c5c3be", roughness: 0.55 })`, one accent key `k.material("plastic", { color: "#d4b018" })`; dials `brushed-metal` (anodised look). No clearcoat.
- Light: `k.rig("top-soft", { dir: [-0.6, 0.9, 0.5], shadowSoftness: 8 })`, key to fill about 3:1, `k.ground({ color: "#f0ede5" })` as a real floor (matte, no reflection), `environment: "soft"`, `lens: [[0, 85]]`, `fstop: 5.6`.
- Camera keys: locked, or `pos: [[0, [0, 0.6, 3.2]], [5, [0.55, 0.6, 3.15], "sine.inOut"]]` for the 12 degree lateral move; `target` on the object. Exploded view: modules separate 30 mm (`0.03` world units at unitsPerMeter 1) in `pose` with `k.prog(t, 1, 1.8, "power2.inOut")` then rejoin.

## How to instruct a model to build it
"Build a product film in the manner of Dieter Rams at Braun. Ground #f0ede5, housing greys #c5c3be and #8a8a87, dark #2e2e2c, ink #1a1a18. One accent only, #d4b018 on the single control the story is about. Labels in Inter Tight 500, 2.8 percent of short side, tracking 0.02em, aligned to the control positions. The product is a rectangle with 8 px radius, matte, soft contact shadow only. Motion: still frame holds 1.4 s; the one control lights in 0.2 s power2.out; a dial rotates 72 degrees over 0.9 s power2.inOut; labels fade 12 frames apart. No gradients, glass, glow, bounce or blur." Claude tends to add a glass or gradient premium layer: strip it. GPT-style output often invents a reflective floor: ban reflections. ## Blending notes
Carries: colour as signal (the ET66's yellow equals key, because it is the most used), matte surface, honest function, modular grid; the Vitsoe principles "unobtrusive", "honest" and "as little design as possible" are the test. Blends with `muji` (shared emptiness, different warmth), `swiss-international` and `ulm-braun-functionalism` (grid), `nhk-broadcast-design` (observation of objects), `teenage-engineering` (playful descendant). Dilutes badly with maximalism; do not add bursts, grain or neon.
