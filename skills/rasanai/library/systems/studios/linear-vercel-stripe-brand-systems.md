## What it is
Three software brands whose design systems became the default reference for how serious developer and fintech products look. They share restraint (one accent or none, strong type, hairlines) but differ in temperature: Linear is dark and engineered, Vercel is monochrome and Swiss-leaning, Stripe is light, navy-inked and warm in its gradients. Unlike the first version of this entry, the colour and type tokens below were read from each brand's live CSS custom properties (fetched 2026-10-03), so hexes are exact for that date; they will drift as the sites are redesigned.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
Linear
- Colour is generated in LCH from three variables (base, accent, contrast), replacing 98 (Linear's UI post). In practice the dark ladder is: marketing floor #010102, app primary #08090a, then #0f1011, #141516, #191a1b as bg-level-0 to 3, and #1c1c1f, #232326, #28282c as secondary to quaternary. Dark text is near white; Mercury White #f4f5f8 and Nordic Gray #222326 are named in the brand page.
- Accent is a single desaturated indigo: #5e6ad2 (brand-bg), #7170ff for the dark UI accent, hover #828fff, tint #18182f. It appears only on the mark, focus, selection and one primary action.
- Borders do the work shadows do elsewhere: #23252a / #34343a / #3e3e44, plus white at 5, 8, 12 percent.
- Type: Inter Variable with cv01 and ss03 feature settings (single-storey a, a flatter tail), Inter Display for headings; body 15 px at 1.6; display tracking -0.022em; mono is Berkeley Mono; Tiempos Headline is declared for rare serif use.
- Motion tokens in its CSS: base duration .18 s, quick transition .1 s, and a full named ease table (out-expo cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1), out-quint (.23,1,.32,1), in-out-quart (.77,0,.175,1)). This is the strongest sourced timing in the entry.
Vercel (Geist)
- Geist is by Vercel with Basement Studio and Andres Briganti: Geist Sans (geometric, Swiss-inspired), Geist Mono, Geist Pixel; influences named: Inter, Univers, SF Mono, SF Pro, Suisse International, ABC Diatype and ABC Diatype Mono; SIL OFL 1.1 (geist-font repo).
- Backgrounds are #ffffff / #fafafa (light) and #000000 (dark); gray-1000 is #171717 on light and #ededed on dark; gray-900 #4d4d4d light, #a0a0a0 dark; gray-600 #878787 / #a8a8a8; gray-100 #f2f2f2 / #1a1a1a; blue-700 #0070f7 (blue-600 #0090ff). The Geist docs list ten scales (backgrounds, gray, gray-alpha, blue, red, amber, green, teal, purple, pink) with steps 100 to 1000 and a defined role per step (100 default background, 900 secondary text, 1000 primary text and icons).
Stripe
- Brand scale (CSS tokens): brand-600 #533afd, brand-500 #665efd, brand-700 #4032c8, brand-900 #1c1e54, brand-25 #f5f5ff; neutral-990 #061b31 (ink), neutral-800 #273951, neutral-500 #64748d, neutral-25 #f8fafd; ruby #ea2261, orange #ff6118, magenta #f44bcc / #ffd7ef, lemon #f9b900. #635bff is still present (focus border). Warm hues live inside the gradient and illustrations; UI chrome stays indigo and navy.
- Accent gradient stops in its CSS: #bdb4ff, #643afd, #533afd.
- Type: sohne-var (Klim, licensed), weight 300 on display, stack falls back to SF Pro Display.
- The animated hero is WebGL ("minigl" and a Gradient class) in a canvas inside a container with --section-skew-Y -12deg; the tutorial's four demo colours (#6ec3f4, #3a3aff, #ff61ab, #E63946) are the demo's, not Stripe's current palette. Hufnagl notes he could not fully decode the noise and speed.

## Tokens decoded
- Dark (Linear-like): canvas #010102; surfaces #0f1011, #141516, #191a1b; text #f7f8f8 (proposed, near Mercury White #f4f5f8); muted text proposed #8a8f98; accent #5e6ad2; border rgba(255,255,255,0.08).
- Light (Vercel-like): #ffffff, #fafafa, ink #171717, secondary #4d4d4d, borders #eaeaea, link blue #0070f7.
- Light (Stripe-like): page #ffffff or #f8fafd, ink #061b31, secondary #64748d, button #533afd, deep band #1c1e54.
- Type: Inter at opsz 32+, 500 or 600, size 56 px (5.2% of 1080), line-height 1.1, tracking -0.022em; body Inter 400 at 15 px / 1.6 / -0.011em; mono Geist Mono 12 px caps +0.04em. Stripe variant: Inter 300.
- Radius 8 px cards, 6 px controls (proposed), 1 px borders; no shadows on dark.

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames, GSAP): panels rise `y: 12 -> 0`, `opacity 0 -> 1`, 350 ms `expo.out` (Linear's own ease-out-expo curve is cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1)), stagger 60 ms; small state changes (hover, toggle, row highlight) 100 to 180 ms `power2.out`; hero UI push 1.00 -> 1.04 over 6 s `sine.inOut`; cursor moves 600 to 900 ms `power2.inOut` along a curved path with a 250 ms click ring (screen-studio-cursor-zoom for the camera). Numbers count with `expo.out`, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Gradient mesh (Stripe-like) is three or four blurred blobs (`filter: blur(60px)`, seeded, drifting 10 to 20 px over 12 s, `sine.inOut`) inside a `transform: skewY(-12deg)` band with `overflow: hidden`; or a canvas shader sampled on the integer frame.
3D (Rasan3D): a UI card in space, never glossy. `k.panel({ width: 1.6, height: 1, depth: 0.02, radius: 0.03, texture: k.image("assets/ui.png") })` (unlit face keeps the real UI colours), `camera.lens: [[0, 35]]`, `pos` arc 12 to 20 degrees over 2.4 s `power3.inOut`, `fstop: 5.6` so the slab stays sharp, rig `three-point` at low intensity, `environment: "soft"`, no bloom except one `k.material("emissive")` accent at intensity 2. Gradient mesh as a post-free background pass: `k.pass("mesh", { at: "scene", blend: "replace", uniforms: { uA: "#533afd", uB: "#ff6118", uC: "#f44bcc", uD: "#bdb4ff" }, frag: ... })` using `#include <r3/noise>` `r3Fbm2(vUv * 1.4 + vec2(0.0, uTime * 0.05), 4)` fed to `r3Ramp4`, then `r3Dither` at 255 levels to prevent banding (declare intent since a flat-ish field can trigger blank-frame). Exploded UI layers: stacked `k.panel`s with z offsets 0.05 to 0.3 m, separated in 0.7 s `expo.out`, stagger 80 ms.

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready (Linear-like): "Dark software launch. Canvas #010102; surfaces #0f1011, #141516, #191a1b; no drop shadows; borders rgba(255,255,255,0.08); one accent #5e6ad2 on a single CTA and focus rings only. Inter (opsz 32, weight 500) display at 5% of frame height, tracking -0.022em, line-height 1.1; body Inter 15 px / 1.6; labels Geist Mono 12 px caps. Panels (radius 8 px) rise 12 px with fade in 350 ms expo.out, stagger 60 ms; micro-interactions 180 ms. Real product data. Locked camera with a 2 to 4 percent zoom." Stripe variant: "page #ffffff, ink #061b31, Inter 300 display tracking -0.02em, one filled button #533afd, a soft four-colour mesh (ruby #ea2261, orange #ff6118, magenta #f44bcc, indigo #533afd) in a band skewed -12 degrees behind the header only." Vercel variant: "black or white only, Geist 500 display, Geist Mono labels, gray ramp #ededed/#a0a0a0/#878787, one blue #0070f7 for links." Claude over-decorates with glows and extra badges; cap glow at one element. GPT drifts to purple-blue blobs; list the allowed colours and forbid others.

## Blending notes
Carries: one accent, surface lifts and translucent borders, tight tracking, real product UI, fast eases. Linear plus Apple gives dark premium software; Stripe plus Swiss gives fintech clarity; Vercel plus terminal-crt gives developer-infrastructure. Breaks against heavy texture, handmade or warm craft looks, and ornamental type.
Also clashes with (not library entries): maximalist neon.
