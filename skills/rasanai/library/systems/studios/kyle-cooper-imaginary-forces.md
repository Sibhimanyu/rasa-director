## What it is
Kyle Cooper (MFA Yale, mentored by Paul Rand; he names Rand over Saul Bass as his main influence, Thunder Chunky interview) designed the Se7en title sequence at R/Greenberg Associates for David Fincher's 1995 film. Where title design was drifting toward digital compositing, it was made as tabletop photography, hand-scratched type on scratchboard and an optical printer, deliberately keeping the machine's accidents. Cooper's stated aim: "I wanted it to be like the killer did the film opticals himself" (Art of the Title). With Peter Frankfurt and Chip Houghton he founded Imaginary Forces on October 31, 1996 (Motionographer); the studio also made the 2007 Mad Men titles, a cool, flat, Bass-descended register that is a different system.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- Make the thing, then damage it. Se7en lettering was scrawled into scratchboard and shot on film; type was manipulated in film transfer, hand-drawn letters were mixed with Helvetica; the result was cut and reassembled (Art of the Title). Cooper: "people think there's computer graphics in there, but we assembled the majority of the sequence by hand" (via The Fincher Analyst, 2010 Blu-ray interview).
- Real objects with menace, filmed in camera: fish hooks, razor blades, sewing needles, twine, hair, personal photographs, handmade journals (The Fincher Analyst; Art of the Title). The close-up of an object is the unit of the piece.
- Keep the accident: Art of the Title records preserved optical-printer "accidents" (fish hooks and razor blades in the machinery). Damage must look caused, not overlaid.
- End credits as a physical artefact: each name cut out and taped into one continuous scroll, shot on video, backlit so light bled through the letters, studded with objects, and rolled downward not upward (The Fincher Analyst).
- Cut to the music's pulse: Angus Wall's edit to the Coil remix (credited "Danny Hide") of Nine Inch Nails' "Closer"; the track's texture is described as nail-on-chalkboard.
- Mad Men mode (Imaginary Forces, about 38 to 40 s): "almost monochrome", right-angle graphic world, multiple static camera angles in the manner of live action; skyscrapers in After Effects 3D, falling figure in Softimage; RJD2 "A Beautiful Mine"; creative directors Steve Fuller and Mark Gardner (Art of the Title).

- Mad Men mode (measured from a contact sheet, a mix of frames): #cccec5 and #a5a499 light greys, #7b7e76 and #535149 mid, #080808 black, #a37a60 warm accent at 4.6% (skin and period ad colour). No gradients.
- Type: scrawl layer in Rock Salt or Reenie Beanie rotated within +/-2 degrees, 6 to 9% of frame height, uneven baseline per letter; clean layer in Inter Tight or Archivo (wdth tweened toward condensed), uppercase, +0.04em tracking, 2 to 3% of frame height; Special Elite for notebook annotations.
- Texture: grain 8 to 14% visible, dust and hairs on 2 of 8 frames, leader marks at cuts (proposed, from vocabulary.md texture ranges).

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames, GSAP): drive every layer from a quantised clock so the film runs on twos. `const f = Math.floor(t*30); const held = f - (f % 2) + (hash(f>>1) < 0.2 ? -1 : 0)` using a seeded mulberry32 on the integer frame (irregular holds are the point; perfectly even jitter is the tell). Per held frame: `x +/-3 px, rotation +/-0.4 deg` (wiggle), duplicate layer smear 6 to 14 px at 40% opacity for exactly one frame. Scratch-in reveal: `clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0) -> inset(0)` in 66 to 133 ms `steps(3)` jumps, edge roughened by an `feTurbulence` (seeded, step the seed every held frame) into `feDisplacementMap` scale 5 to 12; `steps(1)` entrances, hard cuts, no easing on type. Damage behaviours to pick from (use at most two for the whole piece): displacement boil, one-frame smear, flash-frame (full-frame brightness +40% for 1 frame), leader stripe. Gate weave 0.3 to 1 px, halation off. Cut to the beat list with the hit frame on the object, not the title.
Se7en 3D reading (Rasan3D, tabletop): `lens: [[0, 100]]` mm, `fstop: 2.8` with `focus: [[0, d1], [1.2, d2, "power2.inOut"]]` for rack focus between objects; objects as `k.THREE` boxes and planes with `k.material("paper")` notebooks and `k.image(url)` photographs, razor/hook as small `k.material("brushed-metal")` meshes; rig `low-key` with `dir` about 20 degrees off the table plane for raking shadow; `k.ground({ y: 0, color: "#010302" })`. Push 4 to 8% in 1.5 to 2.5 s on `power2.inOut`, hold, cut. Stepped time: in `pose(t, k)` read `const tt = Math.floor(t * 15) / 15` plus a `k.hash(frame, seed)` extra hold; set `motionBlur: false` (blur contradicts the transferred, stepped look) and declare it with `declare.intent` stating why. Post: `post: { grain: 0.04, vignette: 0.3, saturation: 0.6 }`; or a `k.pass("damage", { at: "post", ... })` that displaces `r3Src(uv)` by `r3Value2` noise and runs `r3Grain(c, gl_FragCoord.xy, float(uFrame), 0.06)`.
Mad Men 3D reading: flat `k.panel`s with graph-paper textures at 1 px lines on a 24 px cell, locked camera at lens 35 mm, a figure falling in a straight drop of 1.6 to 2.4 s `power1.in` (accelerating), static camera cuts between 3 to 4 angles.

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready brief: "Kyle Cooper / Se7en language. Shoot an invented set of physical objects on a dark table (named: razor blade, fish hook, photographs, notebook page; no generic vignette). Canvas #010302, ink greys #151510 to #66675c, highest tone #97a19b, no saturated colour except one dirty red object under 5% of frame. Titles are scratched strokes in off-white-grey on black with rough edges, mixed with one clean grotesque line (Inter Tight caps +0.04em). The entire piece animates on twos with seeded irregular holds; jitter +/-3 px, +/-0.4 degrees; exactly two damage behaviours (displacement boil and one-frame smear) used consistently. Edit staccato to the beat list. Grain 10%, dust, leader marks at cuts. Hard cuts only." Claude over-stacks effects; cap at two. GPT defaults to a generic dark vignette with a stock grunge font; require named objects and a rough-stroke path for letters.

## Blending notes
Carries: damage as authorship, twos timing with irregular holds, tabletop macro, objects as symbols. Mix with Bass for one clear metaphor, with Ferro for rapid montage, with VHS for transfer damage. Breaks with soft UI gradients, Apple product light and Swiss order. Mad Men mode is the opposite temperature (cool, geometric, silent camera): do not mix the two modes in one piece.
