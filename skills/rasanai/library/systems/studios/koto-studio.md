## What it is
Koto is an international brand and design studio (London, Berlin, New York, Los Angeles, Sydney; "optimism, collaboration, and craft", koto.com). Founders per secondary sources: James Greenfield, Jowey Roden, Caroline Matthews, 2015 (not stated on the pages fetched). Its work is large-platform identity: Amazon (18 months, 15 markets, 50+ sub-brands, per its case study), Instagram wordmark, Lyft (2025), Deezer (2023), Adobe, Coinbase, Microsoft Copilot, WhatsApp, Fitbit, Faculty, Klarna. What it contributes to a blend is not a look but a method: one owned colour, a custom typeface, a logo that behaves, and components that stretch.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- One hue per client, pushed: Deezer Purple (#a136fc measured from its logo GIF) with red, blue, green, black and white as support; Lyft "a distinctive bright pink" across billboards, app and cars; Amazon "Smile Orange" replacing several oranges plus a more saturated Prime Blue (case studies).
- A custom type system is part of the identity: Ember Modern (364 languages) and Amazon Logo Sans; Deezer Sans, a variable font whose letterforms echo the logo; a custom Lyft typeface.
- The logo leads the system: Deezer's logo is a heart that pulses, and "dynamic beats" turn into graphic patterns, illustration, sound and container shapes (Creative Review). Creative director Joe Ling: "our logo lead system".
- Motion is designed as three tiers: animated brand assets, a motion system of traits and behaviours (Faculty: "balanced", "straightforward", "bright"), then guidelines; "bad motion is worse than no motion at all" (Koto newsletter).
- Evolution over revolution: Lyft "grow up without blending in"; the Amazon arrow gets "a warmer smile".
- The studio brands itself loudly on purpose: yellow everywhere including invoices, against the black-and-white norm of design studios; "uncompromising positivity" (Greenfield, The Brand Identity). Their 2025 site inverts to near-black with the yellow as the only colour.
- Not documented: Koto's typeface for its own site, grid numbers, ms timings. Those below are proposed.

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames/GSAP): the mark acts first. A pulse: `scale` 1 to 1.12 to 1 in 420 ms `power2.out` then `power2.in`, repeated on the beat grid; the same pulse drives container shapes (scale 0.9 to 1 stagger 60 ms) and a pattern field (rows of the device offset by one beat each). Wordmark assembly: parts scale 0.6 to 1 with rotation 12 to 0 degrees, 450 ms `back.out(1.2)`, 50 ms stagger. Headline: rise 32 px with a `wght` tween 400 to 700 over 450 ms `power3.out`. Holds 900 ms. Slide between states 350 ms. Overshoot small: positivity, not slapstick. Sound: one tonal hit per pulse (vocabulary: Sound).

3D (Rasan3D): a soft hero object (`k.material("clay", { color: "#a136fc" })` or `ceramic`), `k.rig("top-soft", { shadows: true, shadowSoftness: 10 })`, `k.ground({ y: 0, shadowOpacity: 0.25 })` over a flat 2D accent ground, `environment: "soft"`, lens `[[0, 50]]`, `fstop: 4`. The object pulses scale 1 to 1.08 on the beat inside `pose` via `k.prog`; the camera does one `sine.inOut` 12 degree `k.orbit` over 4 s. Type stays DOM above (`k.pinDom` only if it must sit on a face). Avoid chrome and bloom.

## How to instruct a model to build it
"Platform rebrand reveal in the manner of Koto. Pick ONE owned colour (e.g. #a136fc) as a full-bleed ground; ink white and black only. The logo is the system: build it from three shape groups that assemble in 450 ms back.out(1.2) at 50 ms stagger, then pulse (scale 1 to 1.12, 420 ms) on every beat of the track. Let that pulse drive the next elements: a field of the logo's own shape repeated, then four reusable components (card, badge, button, tag, radius 20 px) sliding in 120 ms apart. Headline in Bricolage Grotesque 700 with a wght tween, one promise only, 8 percent of short side. Hold the end frame 900 ms. No gradients, no glass, no glow, no stock pastel blobs. Paused timeline only; pulses are functions of the beat grid." Claude tends to list features, so cut to one promise and four components. GPT-style output often adds soft pastel blobs and a second accent; ban both.

## Blending notes
Carries: single owned accent, logo-led motion, component thinking, variable-type motion, friendly assembly. Blends with Collins (entertainment energy), Wolff Olins (idea-led reveals), DIA (type layer), Spotify Wrapped (beat-driven systems). Clashes with Braun, Sagmeister and any grim system; do not mix with a second loud hue.
