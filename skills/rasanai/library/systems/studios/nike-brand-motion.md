## What it is
Nike's film and print voice is a compact system: the Swoosh (Carolyn Davidson, a Portland State design student, first used 18 June 1971, paid $35), "Just Do It" (coined 1988 by Dan Wieden of Wieden+Kennedy), Futura Extra Bold Condensed in capitals, black and white with orange held back, and sports footage cut for rhythm and match. This entry captures the typographic and filmic grammar; it is not Nike's brand guideline, which was not available. The strongest recent specimen is "You Can't Stop Us" (W+K Portland, July 2020, 90 s), where the idea is carried by editing alone.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- Typeface: Futura Extra Bold Condensed, uppercase. Fonts In Use lists it with Palatino and Helvetica Now on Nike's 2025 "So Win" campaign. A bespoke "Futura ND Nike 365" and a 1988 launch in Futura Condensed Extra Bold are reported by secondary sources only (not re-fetched this session).
- Type is a caption of intent: 1 to 6 words, anchored to an edge, never a centred slide title.
- The film is built by matching movement, not subject: in "You Can't Stop Us" two clips are paired so action on the left continues on the right, "a complete frame that looks as if it was one shot" (Designboom). 4,000+ archive clips were reviewed over months with sourcing agency Stalkr; 72 clips, 36 split-screen pairs, 24 sports, 53 athletes (Designboom, It's Nice That).
- Most of it was done in camera: five shoot days, a custom camera frame emulating the archive angles, lighting and grade matched to archive; Hudson called it "shooting ultra prescriptive half-frames" (It's Nice That). The cost of the effect is research and matching, not a transition preset.
- Honest register: grain, desaturation, contrast, sweat; no cinematic gloss.
- Credits to cite: director Oscar Hudson (Pulse Films), editors Peter Wiedensmith and Jessica Baclesse, DP Logan Triplett, VFX A52, narration Megan Rapinoe (It's Nice That, Designboom). Cannes Grand Prix and Emmy claims came from a search summary only: unverified.

## Tokens decoded
- Canvas #111111, paper #ffffff, orange #fa5400 for one packaging or CTA beat. All proposed (see palette evidence).
- Display: Barlow Condensed 800, uppercase, tracking 0 to +0.01em, line-height 0.9, cap height about 9 to 14 percent of frame height. Tween the weight only with Oswald (`font-variation-settings: "wght"` 400 to 700); Barlow is static weights.
- Placement: lower-left or flush to an edge with a 4 to 6 percent margin (proposed).
- Image grade (vocabulary.md section 4 terms): contrast +20 to 30 percent, saturation -30 to -100 percent, grain 6 to 10 percent, crushed blacks, no teal-orange.

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames, GSAP):
- Text stamp: opacity 0 to 1 in one frame (`ease: "steps(1)"`) with scale 1.04 to 1 over 250 ms `power4.out`. Words land on VO syllables, 100 to 250 ms apart.
- Split screen: two or three `<video>` panels in `overflow:hidden` boxes with a 0 to 2 px seam; the reveal is a `clip-path: inset()` wipe 300 to 500 ms `power3.inOut`. Eye-match by placing the key joint or ball at the same screen coordinate in both panels (set `object-position` per clip). Hold the pair 1.0 to 1.5 s, then hard cut.
- Speed ramp: `playbackRate` or a time-remap 100 percent to 25 to 100 over 400 to 600 ms, centred on the impact frame; add 2 to 3 frames of directional blur.
- Handheld energy: seeded jitter, plus or minus 3 px at 8 to 12 Hz, never `Math.random`.
- Cuts: hard, or whip-pan match cut 120 to 250 ms with horizontal blur (see whip-pan-match-cut).
3D (Rasan3D; read references/3d.md sections 3, 5, 12):
- Product in a black void: `Rasan3D.stage({ background: "#111111", environment: "studio", toneMapping: "neutral", motionBlur: { shutter: 0.5 }, post: { grain: 0.03, vignette: 0.25, saturation: 0.8 }, camera: { pos: [[0,[0.4,0.3,2.2]],[1.2,[0.1,0.2,1.6],"power4.out"]], target: [[0,[0,0.1,0]]], lens: [[0,35]], fstop: 2.8 } })`.
- Light: `k.rig("low-key", { dir: [-1, 1.2, 0.8], shadows: true })` for one hard key, then a single back rim; material `k.material("plastic", { color: "#ffffff" })` or `matte`; no bloom on white.
- Caps type: `k.extrudeText("JUST DO IT", { font: <Barlow Condensed TTF>, size, depth: 0.04, bevel: 0.004 })` for the 3D version, or a DOM stamp via `k.pinDom` for crispness. Camera whip: a `pos` key with `"power4.inOut"` over 250 ms, with shutter 0.5 for the blur.

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready: "Nike-style athletic film. Footage desaturated 40 to 100 percent, contrast up, grain 8 percent, no teal-orange. Text is Barlow Condensed 800, uppercase, cap height 9 to 14 percent of frame, line-height 0.9, flush to an edge with a 5 percent margin, 1 to 6 words, stamped on a cut (1 frame) with a 250 ms power4.out scale settle from 1.04. Cuts land on voiceover syllables. Where two actions rhyme, use split screen with a 1 px seam and eye-match the key point to the same screen position. One speed ramp on impact (100, 25, 100 percent over 500 ms). One orange beat at most. No shadows, outlines or gradients on type."
Claude tends to ease everything softly; demand hard cuts and stamps and name the eases. GPT-style output often picks an unrelated bold font; name the font, weight and cap height (observed tendency, not tested here).

## Blending notes
Carries: caps and edge anchoring, split-screen matching, desaturated contrast, percussive cuts. Mixes with Pablo Ferro's montage grammar, Swiss grids and DIA's modular type. Breaks with soft pastel motion, with Apple's calm cadence and with Muji's emptiness. Also clashes with (no library entry): pastel-soft.
