## What it is
Maurice Binder (born New York, 4 December 1925; died London, 9 April 1991) worked mainly in Britain from the 1950s and designed the title sequences of the Bond films from Dr. No (1962) to Licence to Kill (1989), with Robert Brownjohn doing From Russia with Love and Goldfinger and Daniel Kleinman taking over at GoldenEye (1995). Sources disagree on the count: Art of the Title says 14 Bond films, Wikipedia says 16; its list runs Dr. No, then Thunderball onward through Licence to Kill, so the number depends on whether the Brownjohn films and Dr. No's credit split are counted. He summed up the work as "girls, guns, smoke, and steam" (Art of the Title). The reusable core is graphic: a fixed opening ritual, silhouettes on saturated fields, optical mattes, distorted type, all cut to a theme song.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- A ritual opening. The gun barrel for Dr. No: Binder first planned to film through a real .38 barrel; the lens would not focus the whole bore, so his assistant Trevor Bond built a pinhole camera (Wikipedia). Binder said he did the idea "in a hurry" and used "little white, price tag stickers" as the dots, then Bond fires and red runs down the screen (Wikipedia). The dots are cheap, hand-placed marks, not clean vector circles.
- Dr. No's own sequence is minimalist graphic animation with coloured dots, squares and silhouettes, hand-coloured black-and-white film and rephotographed negatives (Art of the Title; animation by Trevor Bond, 1.66:1).
- Silhouettes on a coloured backdrop begin with Thunderball (1965), where optical compositing of separate negatives first appears, with rippling kinetic type; Little White Lies calls it "the idea" he struck gold with, and credits it fully in On Her Majesty's Secret Service (1969). Brownjohn's lineage is different: credits projected onto bodies, and in Goldfinger characters superimposed on a gold-painted model (Little White Lies).
- Optical craft by film (Art of the Title): You Only Live Twice windows cut from silhouettes over volcanic imagery; On Her Majesty's Secret Service scale play (Bond on a clock hand) and op-art distortion; Diamonds Are Forever mattes that enclose women in diamonds; Live and Let Die type warped as if seen through water and a burning skull; The Man with the Golden Gun refraction through rippling water pools; The Spy Who Loved Me ultra-slow-motion silhouettes; Moonraker zero-gravity orientation; Octopussy lasers on bodies and smoke; A View to a Kill neon paint under blacklight; The Living Daylights water distortion with a softer red, blue and yellow overlap; Licence to Kill digital compositing and nonlinear editing.
- Recurring motifs: silhouetted hands, water distortion, eye close-ups, the 007 logo as key art (Art of the Title).
- Rhythm follows the theme song, with cuts on its hits.

## Tokens decoded
- Ground: #070405 (51% of the Dr. No card; do not use #000000 flat, the card carries a faint warm cast). Plum shadow #363145. Gunshot red #660707 (deep, not fire-engine). Dots: blue #1777c8, green #1ab027, magenta-grey #967691 (all measured from the card).
- Montage tones: grey ladder #232127, #474649, #7e7d7c; red #982b17; blue #2f3da9. One saturated field per sequence for a blend; the orange #f26a1b and magenta #d6247a field options are proposed.
- Type: Jost 700 or Josefin Sans 600, uppercase, +0.08em, 6 to 9% of frame height, one credit at a time; ripple with an SVG `feTurbulence` (`baseFrequency` 0.01 0.02, seed stepped on the integer frame) into `feDisplacementMap` scale 12 to 24, animated through the credit's life.
- Aperture: circular mask, hard edge, grows from 0.5% to 100% of frame diagonal.
- Dots: hand-cut discs of slightly varied diameter (8 to 12 px at 1080p), each 1 to 2 px off a perfect line, marching 24 frames, moving right in a procession (proposed from observation of the sequence's structure; no measured frame count taken).

## Motion and camera
2D (HyperFrames, GSAP): dots as absolutely positioned `div`s on a timeline `x: -40 -> 1960` at `ease: "none"` (a procession is constant speed), halt on a held cut, then `clip-path: circle(0.5% at 50% 50%)` to `circle(70% at 50% 50%)` over 18 frames `power2.in` for the barrel; flood: a red `div` with `scaleY: 0 -> 1`, `transform-origin: top`, 18 frames `power2.in`, drips by 3 to 5 offset thin bars at differing speeds. Silhouettes: black SVG shapes on a colour field, a looping 0.5 to 1 Hz undulation `x/rotation` on `sine.inOut` (yoyo), not a bouncing loop; credits are children of the figure's group so they ride its transform. Water ripple: animated `feDisplacementMap` seed stepped each frame under a `steps()` ease; refraction look: duplicate the field, blur 3 px, displace, blend `screen` at 30%. Hard cuts on brass hits (beat list), no hold over 2 s, no crossfades except cross-dissolved vignettes in the 1980s mode. Never idle: silhouettes move even on the hold.
3D (Rasan3D): backlit cut-outs in depth. Each silhouette a `k.pxPlane`/plane with `k.image("assets/sil-01.png")` (alpha PNG), `k.material("unlit")` black or `emissive` for lit rims, planes 0.2 to 0.5 m apart for parallax; the colour field an `unlit` background plane or `background: "#982b17"` on the stage; camera locked at `lens: [[0, 50]]` with `pos` drifting 0.1 m over 6 s `sine.inOut` (declare the intent, the slow drift is the point); `post: { grain: 0.02, vignette: 0.25 }`. Water: a `k.pass("ripple", { at: "post", uniforms: { uAmp: (t) => 0.012 * Math.min(1, t) }, frag })` that samples `r3Src(vUv + uAmp * vec2(sin(vUv.y*40.0 + uTime*2.0), cos(vUv.x*30.0 + uTime*1.6)))`. Blacklight (1985 look): thin line-art as `k.material("emissive")` at intensity 3 on a black ground with `post: { bloom: { strength: 0.8, threshold: 0.2 } }`. Gun barrel in 3D: a `k.tube` bore viewed straight down with `lens: [[0, 35]]`, a rifled interior via `r3Engrave`, dots as an instanced row.

## How to instruct a model to build it
"Bond-style opening, graphic not photoreal. Ground #070405 with one saturated field per sequence (deep red #982b17 or blue #2f3da9). Open with a procession of hand-cut white dots crossing black at constant speed, then a circular aperture, then a flood of #660707 running down the frame. Main imagery is black silhouettes (generic figures, hands, abstract shapes, no real likenesses or branding) undulating at 0.7 Hz on sine.inOut over the field, enclosed by optical mattes (circle, diamond). Credits are Jost 700 caps, +0.08em, one at a time, parented to the figure, with a water-ripple displacement that settles in 12 frames. Cut on the theme's downbeats; hold none longer than 2 s." Claude should avoid inventing franchise branding; use abstract silhouettes. GPT tends to add sexualised stock figures and neon synthwave outlines: forbid the neon unless the 1985 blacklight mode is chosen explicitly.

## Blending notes
Carries: silhouette on a saturated field, mattes, ritual opening, ripple on type, music-tied cuts. Blends with Bass (cut-out clarity), mid-century modern (flat shape and colour) and Kyle Cooper (the damaged-optical lineage). Breaks with data-heavy or photoreal imagery. Reuse the structure (silhouette, field, aperture) and not the content: the original relies on objectified bodies, which a modern film does not need.
