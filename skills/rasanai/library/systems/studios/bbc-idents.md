## What it is
Martin Lambie-Nairn designed the Channel 4 "Blocks" ident (1982), BBC Two's 1991 idents, and the 1997 BBC One red hot-air balloon (Wikipedia). He pioneered applying branding to screen media with computer technology and wrote Brand Identity for Television: With Knobs On (1997). This entry covers three BBC eras: BBC Two 1991 to 2001, BBC One "Rhythm & Movement" 2002 to 2006 and BBC One "Circle" 2006 to 2016.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- BBC Two (16 February 1991 to 19 November 2001): a sans-serif '2' in Gill Sans, in viridian, in a variety of environments. Commissioned by controller Alan Yentob because the earlier look was "too dull and worthy" and to make the channel appear less stuffy. The '2' was "rather nice and fat" with "very sharp bits". Techniques: paint splashing, water, metal impalement, neon, copper sparks, glass reflection, silk, powder, optical fibre, toys, firecrackers. 1991 to 1997: 14 idents; 1997 to 2001: 9 more; revived 2014 to 2018; succeeded by Curve idents in 2018 (Wikipedia).
- BBC One Rhythm & Movement (29 March 2002 to 7 October 2006): people of varied backgrounds dancing on a predominantly red background to a jingle by Peter Lawlor (Water Music Productions) in string quartet, techno and hip-hop versions. Sixteen primary idents (Capoeira, Ballet, Hip-Hop, Salsa, Haka, Tap Dogs and others); locations from London's skyline and Cornwall's Minack Theatre to Kenya's Maasai Mara; the logo in a red box; the first BBC One look without a station clock.
- BBC One Circle (7 October 2006 to 4 December 2016): Red Bee Media produced it, ending Lambie-Nairn's fifteen years at the BBC; a circle as a symbol of unity and a nod to the classic globe; people, plants or animals form circles; white lines and a red eclipse animate around the circle; lowercase 'one'; music by Imran Hanif; shortened in May 2009 with David Arnold adaptations.
- Lambie-Nairn's method per the Ravensbourne archive: material exploration, movement and rhythm, diversity, content tied to channel demographics.

## Motion and camera
Durations here are proposed; the sources give lists of techniques and credits, not timings.

2D (HyperFrames, craft.md vocabulary): a flat-colour lockup that the action resolves into.
- 0 to 5 s the action runs (paint splash, water, silk fold, copper sparks: as seeded sprite or video layers); at 5 s the numeral or circle forms; hold the lockup 1.0 s.
- Circle device: a white arc draws around the centre (`stroke-dashoffset`, 1.2 s `power2.inOut`), a red ellipse rotates once behind it (`rotate` 0 to 360, 1.8 s `sine.inOut`).
- Resolve: the last 0.4 s eases the camera to a frontal, flat view so the 2D lockup matches exactly (`scale` to 1, `power2.out`), one soft impact on the form, no whoosh. Camera: slow or locked, never handheld. Grade: saturated but clean, no teal-orange.
3D (Rasan3D, 3d.md sections 3, 5, 13, 18): a practical set, a hero material discovered doing something, resolving to the mark.
- Forming by simulation: `const sim = k.simulate("forming", { dt: 1/120, checkpointEvery: 0.5, init(state, k) {...}, step(state, dt, t, k) {...} })` where `init` seeds 4000 particle positions from `k.rng(seed)` and `step` eases each toward its target point (targets from `k.surfacePoints(numeralMesh, 4000, seed)`, stored in `state`); in `pose(t, k)` read `sim.at(t)` and write instance matrices to an `InstancedMesh` of small spheres with `k.material("plastic", { color: "#40826d" })` (3d.md section 13: seek-safe).
- Numeral: `k.extrudeText("2", { font: <TTF>, size: 3, depth: 0.7, bevel: 0.06, material: k.material("ceramic", { color: "#ffffff" }) })`; `k.rig("three-point")` with a softbox key; `background: "#40826d"`; `environment: "soft"`.
- Camera keys: `lens: [[0, 35], [5, 85, "power2.inOut"]]`, `pos` from an angled hero view to a frontal `[0, 0, 12]` at t = 5, `shift` unset, `fstop: 4` with `focus: [[0, "target"]]` and `target` on the forming numeral, hold 1 s at the frontal pose (the orthographic-feeling match to the 2D lockup).

## How to instruct a model to build it
"Build a 7 second channel ident in the manner of BBC Two's 1991 package. Ground #40826d. A fat sans numeral, Cabin 700 or a drawn path, 40 percent of frame height, built from a physical action: paint splashes (seeded) fill the numeral's shape over 4 s, then the paint settles and the numeral snaps to a clean white lockup at 5 s on power2.inOut. Hold the lockup 1.0 s. Provide three variants in the same system (paint, water ripple, silk fold) differing in action only. One colour, one numeral. No lens flare, no gradients, no speed ramp. Every tween on the paused timeline; randomness from a seeded generator on the frame index." Claude tends to deliver one animation: ask for the family. GPT-style output often over-polishes with gloss: require physical, tactile materials. (Carried from the earlier pass.)

## Blending notes
Carries: one device in many environments, resolve to a frontal lockup, a family of idents (the 1991 package: Ravensbourne lists Paint, Water, Glass, Silk, Neon, Copper Sparks, Blade, Shadow). Blends with `mtv-80s-idents` (variable skin), `nhk-broadcast-design` (object play), `wolff-olins` (identity as system). Breaks with loud typographic poster looks such as `pentagram-paula-scher-and-partners`.
