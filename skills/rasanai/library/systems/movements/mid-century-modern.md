## What it is
Mid-century modern is the design language of about 1945 to the 1970s, defined by clean, simple lines, honest use of material, open plans and ergonomic furniture. Wikipedia names Charles and Ray Eames, Eero Saarinen (Tulip chair, Dulles Airport), Arne Jacobsen, Richard Neutra and Oscar Niemeyer. In graphics it grew out of modernist abstraction brought into commerce: Alvin Lustig (1915-1955) produced over 70 dust jackets for New Directions between 1945 and 1955 with an abstract, art-informed approach; offset lithography made vivid colour cheap in postcards and print. The Eames Office (1943-1988) is the moving-image reference: over 125 short films including Powers of Ten (1968, re-released 1977) and the Mathematica exhibition (1961).

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- Form follows material and function: molded plywood, fibreglass, steel rod, glass walls; the structure is shown.
- Geometry with a curve: orthogonal planes broken by one organic shape (boomerang, kidney, circle).
- Flat colour in earthy saturation, with walnut and cream as ground; accents in mustard, teal, tomato, olive.
- Whitespace is used. Items sit apart; the page breathes.
- Overprint logic: shapes in transparent ink overlap and darken (multiply).
- Illustration is geometric and flat; people reduced to a few shapes.
- Typography is clean (Futura, Helvetica, Univers) and often lowercase; the title is placed off-axis.
- Optimism: the tone is friendly modernism, not austerity.

## Tokens decoded
- Colour: furniture tones measured from photographs: walnut `#6c4926`, plywood `#a67436`, stone `#dad5ce`, deep ink `#22211f`. Print accents proposed (unmeasured): warm paper `#f1e8d4`, mustard `#d9a021`, teal `#2f7f86`, tomato `#d9482b`, olive `#7b7a3a`. Use at most 3 accents per scene; overprint tint = multiply of two.
- Type: Jost (Futura analogue) for display lowercase 700; DM Serif Display for a warm serif headline; Work Sans body at 28-32 px at 1080p; Source Serif 4 for captions. Futura and Helvetica are commercial; free substitutes named above are OFL.
- Grid: 12 columns, 8 px baseline, 80-120 px outer margins, off-centre title at columns 2-7, image at columns 7-12.
- Shape tokens: circle at 0.3-0.5 of the short side; quarter-circle; kidney curve as an SVG path; flat offset shadow 6 px of the walnut at 20% opacity.
- Texture: paper grain at 4-6% with multiply, offset misregistration of 1-2 px on accent layers.

## Motion and camera
- Modular build: shapes enter from off-frame on orthogonal paths (x or y only), 500 ms `power2.out`, with a 1-3 degree rotation settling to 0; stagger 80-120 ms; overshoot is limited to `back.out(1.2)` on circles only.
- Overprint: accent shape slides 300 ms `power2.inOut` over another with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`; the intersection darkens as it passes, which gives the printed feel.
- Eames-style information films: a planimetric top-down stage, objects arranged on a surface, camera locked; counting or scaling by uniform zoom (Powers of Ten): zoom factor 10x every 1.2-1.5 s with `power1.inOut` and a mid-frame readout.
- Type: lowercase titles slide 24 px and fade, 450 ms `power2.out`; hold at least 1 s.
- Transitions: circle wipe (clip-path circle from the subject, 700 ms `power2.inOut`), or a flat colour block sliding across as a wipe.
- Camera 2D: locked, or a slow lateral truck of 5% over 5 s `sine.inOut`.
- 3D reading (Rasan3D; real API, parameters proposed): a molded-shell chair or glass pavilion. Chair: a shell from `ExtrudeGeometry` or a `LatheGeometry` profile in `k.material("ceramic",{color:"#c7c0b8",roughness:0.35})` (fibreglass) or a plywood `k.material("matte",{color:"#a67436",roughness:0.55})` seat; thin steel rod legs via `k.tube([[...],...],{radius:0.012,radial:12})` in `k.material("chrome")`. Light `k.rig("window",{key:"#ffe9c7",fill:"#dfe8ff",dir:[-1,1.2,0.8],intensity:1,shadows:true,shadowSoftness:6})` with a warm key; `environment:"soft"`; `toneMapping:"agx"`, `exposure:1`; `k.ground({y:0,color:"#dad5ce"})` as a flat colour floor (no reflection). Camera: `lens:[[0,50]]`, `fstop:4` (gentle DoF), `pos` = `Rasan3D.orbit({center:[0,0.5,0],radius:3.2,height:1.2,from:-20,to:-5,t0:0,t1:6,ease:"power1.inOut"})`; `shift:[[0,[0,0.05]]]` keeps verticals vertical in the pavilion shot. Powers-of-Ten reading: a camera `pos` dolly with `lens` held at 35mm and `k.prog`-driven scale of nested panels, 10x per 1.2-1.5s.

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready brief:
"Mid-century modern, 1955. Canvas cream #f1e8d4, ink #2a2622, walnut #7a4a2b, accents mustard #d9a021, teal #2f7f86, tomato #d9482b (max three per scene). Flat geometric shapes only: circles, quarter-circles, one kidney curve. Overlaps use mix-blend-mode multiply so intersections darken like overprint. Title in lowercase Jost 700, placed off-axis at columns 2-7 of a 12-column grid with 100px margins; body in Work Sans. About 40% of the frame stays empty. Paper grain 5%. Motion: shapes enter on x or y axes only, 500ms power2.out, 80ms stagger, circles may use back.out(1.2); one circle-wipe transition 700ms power2.inOut; titles slide 24px and fade 450ms power2.out and hold 1s. Camera locked or 5% lateral truck. No gradients, no drop shadows, no atomic starburst wallpaper."
Claude vs GPT: image-prompted models default to 70s avocado and orange; name walnut and cream as the ground. Code models tend to add rounded-card UI; forbid card shadows.

## Blending notes
Carries well: warm earthy palette, off-axis title, overprint, restrained geometry. Pairs with Japanese modern (flat shape and colour) and with Ulm/Braun (take its function and the grid, add mid-century warmth). With Art Deco, use deco's materials sparingly. With punk or psychedelic it fails: the optimism and whitespace disappear. For a tech product, this is the friendly alternative to Swiss.
