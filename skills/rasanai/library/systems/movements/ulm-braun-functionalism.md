## What it is
The Hochschule fur Gestaltung Ulm was founded in 1953 by Inge Aicher-Scholl, Otl Aicher and Max Bill (first rector; he designed the building, completed 1955) and ran until 1968. Under Tomas Maldonado (rector from 1956) it shifted toward systems thinking, integrating sociology, psychology, economics and philosophy with aesthetics and technology. Its collaboration with Braun, where Dieter Rams joined in 1955 and headed design from 1961 until 1997 (Hans Gugelot among the Ulm collaborators), produced products such as the "Snow White's Coffin" radiogram and the Vitsoe 606 shelving. Aicher carried the method into identity: the 1972 Munich Olympics system of grid-based pictograms with an Alpine palette (blue, white, green, orange, silver), 21 sports posters and the Waldi mascot, plus work for Lufthansa and Braun.

## The rules that make it this and not something else
- Design is a system: one grid and one set of rules produce every product, pictogram and page.
- Rams's ten principles: good design is innovative, useful, aesthetic, understandable, unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting, thorough down to the last detail, environmentally friendly, and "less, but better".
- Neutral objects: grey, white and black; colour is functional, reserved for a switch, a state or a warning.
- Honesty: no fake materials, no decorative form that does not serve a function; controls look like what they do.
- Detail: spacing, radii and label placement on a module; even speaker-grille dots are on a grid.
- Pictograms on a fixed construction grid (Munich 1972: 45 and 90 degree angles, consistent stroke), where the Tokyo 1964 system (Masaru Katsumie) was Aicher's reference.
- Typography: grotesque sans, flush left, few sizes, lowercase leanings.
- Calm: the product or page does not shout; it lets the user work.

## Tokens decoded
- Colour: product neutrals measured from Braun photographs: grey `#9f9f9b`, light grey `#d1d3d4`, black `#1a1618`, white `#f5f8f1`, graphite `#2b3038`. Functional orange `#e8590c` (one control or one state per scene) is proposed, as is the Munich-style set (blue `#2d6db5`, green `#4aa06a`, orange, silver `#c5c8cc`, white), which otlaicher.de describes only qualitatively as a rainbow palette.
- Type: Inter Tight, Archivo or Hanken Grotesk 500 for all text; sizes in a 3-step scale (e.g., 24 / 48 / 120 px at 1080p); label type 14-16 px uppercase with +4% tracking for product labels only. Akzidenz-Grotesk and Univers are commercial; Instrument Sans (wdth axis) is the nearest Univers-like OFL face, Hanken Grotesk and Inter Tight the Akzidenz feel; tween `wght` 400 to 500 only, never a bold punch.
- Grid: 8 columns, 8 px baseline, margin 5% (96 px at 1920), module 120 x 120 px; every dimension a multiple of 8.
- Shapes: 2 px strokes, radii of 8 px on large panels, 0 on labels; dot-grid perforation pitch 12 px, dot 5 px.
- Pictogram construction: stroke 8 px at 128 px cell, 45/90 degrees only, round joints sparingly.

## Motion and camera
- Grid-snapped movement: translations move along a single axis only, in multiples of the module, 350 ms `power2.inOut`; arrival with `power3.out` (no overshoot, no bounce).
- Assemblies and exploded views: parts separate along the product axis by 1 module steps, 80 ms stagger, 600 ms `power2.inOut`, then labels draw in (1px leader lines grow 300 ms `power2.out`).
- Controls: a dial rotates in detents (rotate in 15 degree steps, each step 120 ms `power2.out` with a 1-frame hold); the functional colour lights up with a 200 ms opacity rise, no glow.
- Pictograms: strokes draw in 45/90 segments (stroke-dashoffset 300 ms `power2.out`), then the grid fades out to reveal the figure.
- Type: text blocks mask-rise from a baseline (300 ms `power3.out`); lines enter in order of the grid rows with 60 ms stagger.
- Camera 2D: locked, or orthogonal pans at constant speed; no handheld.
- Stepped details (dial detents, bar-by-bar reveals): if rendered as true steps (`steps(1)` or `ease:"none"` holds), set `data-finish-blur="off"` on that scene's frame root so the finish does not average them.
- 3D reading (Rasan3D; real API, parameters proposed): a matte product on a cyclorama. Body from `RoundedBoxGeometry` (via `await k.addon("geometries/RoundedBoxGeometry.js")`) with `k.material("matte",{color:"#d1d3d4",roughness:0.6})` (ABS), faceplate `k.material("brushed-metal",{color:"#9f9f9b"})`, knobs `CylinderGeometry` with `k.material("matte",{color:"#1a1618"})`, one `k.material("emissive",{color:"#e8590c"})` lamp as the single functional colour (emissive is the only thing that blooms: keep `bloom.strength` 0.3 or leave bloom off). Speaker grille: `k.instanceField({geometry:new k.THREE.CylinderGeometry(0.01,0.01,0.02,10),material:k.material("matte",{color:"#2b3038"}),cell:0.04,count:[40,40],plane:"xy",place:(cx,cz,rng,o)=>{o.scale=1}})` on a 12 px-pitch dot grid. Rig `k.rig("top-soft",{key:"#ffffff",fill:"#dfe8ff",intensity:1,shadows:true,shadowSoftness:9})` (key to fill about 3:1), `environment:"soft"`, `k.ground({y:0,color:"#e8e8e4"})` or `background:"#e8e8e4"`. Camera: `lens:[[0,85]]` or `lens` 120 for near-orthographic, `fstop:8`, `pos` `Rasan3D.orbit({center:[0,0.3,0],radius:5,height:0.8,from:0,to:72,t0:0,t1:6,ease:"none"})` (12 degrees per second, constant). Exploded view: parts offset along one axis in `pose` with `k.prog(t,a,b,"power2.inOut")`, labels as `k.pinDom` leader-line cards. Dials step: `knob.rotation.z = -Math.round(k.prog(t,0.5,2.5)*8)/8*Math.PI*1.5` (stepped detents).

## How to instruct a model to build it
Paste-ready brief:
"Ulm/Braun functionalism. Neutral ground #e8e8e4, ink #1c1c1c, white #f5f5f2, grey #9a9a97; exactly one functional accent (#e8590c) used for the one thing that is active or operated, never decoration. 8-column grid, 8px baseline, 96px margins, module 120px; every position and size is a multiple of 8. Type: Inter Tight or Hanken Grotesk, lowercase, flush left ragged right, three sizes only (24 / 48 / 120 px), labels 14px uppercase +4% tracking. No gradients, no gloss, no bevel; 2px strokes, 8px radius on panels. Pictograms built from 45 and 90 degree strokes on a 128px cell. Motion: single-axis moves in module steps, 350ms power2.inOut, arrival power3.out, no overshoot; exploded views separate along one axis with 80ms stagger; dials turn in 15-degree detents of 120ms; labels via 1px leader lines growing 300ms. Camera locked or constant-speed orthogonal pan. Product shots on a neutral ground with a soft contact shadow."
Claude vs GPT: both tend to add an orange button and rounded white cards and call it Braun. Require the grid numbers, one functional colour, and honest controls; remove any gradient.

## Blending notes
Carries well: modular numbers, functional colour, grid-snapped motion, exploded-view logic, flush-left lowercase. Almost a sibling of Swiss International (shared grid and grotesque; Ulm adds the product and systems voice), and sits well with Japanese modern (Muji follows a similar honesty) and mid-century modern (add warmth through walnut). Breaks against ornament and expressive styles: deco, nouveau, psychedelic and punk. If a blend needs emotion, add it through photography and sound, not through decorative form.
